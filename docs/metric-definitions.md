# Metric Definitions

These metrics are calculated from the source fields and form the common commercial measures used across the dashboard.

## Core KPIs

| Metric | Definition | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Total Sales** | `SUM(Sales)` | Total source-defined Sales in the current filter context. |
| **Total Profit** | `SUM(Profit)` | Total source-defined Profit in the current filter context. |
| **Profit Margin** | `Total Profit / Total Sales` | Dataset-defined profitability ratio. |
| **Order Count** | Distinct count of `Order ID` | Number of unique Orders in the current filter context. |
| **Average Order Value (AOV)** | `Total Sales / Order Count` | Average Sales amount per Order. |

## Highlighted Metrics
This section highlights selected non-core metrics used for customer concentration, profitability exposure, and portfolio distribution analysis.

| Metric | Definition | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Average Sales per Customer** | `Total Sales / distinct Customer count` | Average Sales generated per Customer in the current filter context. |
| **Top 10 Customer Sales Share** | Sales generated by the 10 highest-Sales Customers / Total Sales | Measures customer concentration at the top of the portfolio. |
| **Customers to 80% Sales** | Minimum number of Customers, ranked by Sales from highest to lowest, required to cumulatively reach at least 80% of Total Sales | Measures how broadly or narrowly Sales are distributed across the customer base. |
| **Loss-Making Customer Share** | Customers whose aggregated Profit is below zero / Customers in the analysis population | Measures the prevalence of loss-making Customers in the current analysis context. |
| **Customer Quintile Sales Share** | Sales generated by each dynamically ranked 20% Customer group / Total Sales | Customers are ranked by Sales within the current filter context and divided into five similarly sized groups. |

## Metric Assumptions and Limitations

- **Sales is source-defined.** `Total Sales` reflects the `Sales` field provided by the source dataset. No more specific accounting interpretation is assumed here.

- **Profit is source-defined.** The source does not define the underlying Profit type in enough detail to classify it as gross, operating, or net profit.

- **Profit Margin is dataset-defined.** `Total Profit / Total Sales` should therefore be interpreted as a general profitability ratio, not as a specific accounting margin such as gross margin, operating margin, or net margin.

- **AOV is not a price metric.** A change in Average Order Value may reflect product mix, quantity, discounting, pricing, or other order-composition effects, so it should not be interpreted as a change in price alone.

- **Customer portfolio metrics are context-dependent.** Metrics such as Average Sales per Customer, Loss-Making Customer Share, and Customer Quintile Sales Share are evaluated within the current analysis/filter context.

- **Customer ranking metrics are relative to the active population.** Top 10 Customer Sales Share, Customers to 80% Sales, and Customer Quintile Sales Share depend on how Customers rank by Sales within the relevant analysis context.
